# From pull requests to a shared Android codebase

Contributors have joined and stepped back since 2023, and the droidcon Kenya Android app still works as a shared teaching codebase. Volunteer apps rarely outlive their authors. This one went quiet for up to 211 days, tried Kotlin Multiplatform twice, and moved from Kotlin 1.8 to 2.4.

Three speakers walk it in three parts. History: the eras so far, told from the git log, with guesses marked as guesses. About to launch: what the 2026 update has finished and what is still in progress. The future: the improvement plan, an idea the team is not married to, offered as an invitation to make the app your own.

Attendees leave with a checklist for keeping a shared codebase alive, and a way to join this one.

**Level:** Intermediate
**Prerequisites:** Kotlin and Jetpack Compose basics; familiarity with Hilt and Room helps
**Format:** 45 minutes including Q&A; slides with screen recordings and screenshots
**Speakers:** Donald Isoe, Mobile Engineer at Terra and Community Organiser at Kotlin Kenya and Android 254. Annunziata Kinya, Mobile Engineer at Cashia and Organiser at DroidconKE. Ian Nthuli, Mobile Engineer at NCBA Bank and Organiser at DroidconKE.
